Browser test suite

The same seven test groups for every browser: engine, MDN features, reported capabilities, audio, media, text, and video. Download one results file.
Experiment: pi-suite-v2. Every group records a collection outcome and reason. Video testing can take several minutes. Results are observations, not a pass/fail verdict.
A timeout means “no answer within the waiting limit,” never “feature absent.” Waiting limits are provisional for the pilot and are identical across browsers for each test.
